# System Flow

## Purpose

Define the complete processing sequence for the AI Lead Intake and Qualification Automation.

```text

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
Calculate Lead Score
      ↓
Assign Priority
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
Processing Complete
